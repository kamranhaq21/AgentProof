## Verdict

Every epic's Requirements lines are covered by at least one entry, every `covers` id exists in its epic, every tracer bullet is recorded as a dated Decision, the refactor-sweep rule is honored or a Decision explains why not (epics 7 and 8), and every leaf file's `id`, `title`, `after`, and `hitl` match its `tickets.toml` entry. The remaining findings are wording alignments and confirmations the user owes: `refined: true` on approved stories, and five spine assumptions that story criteria already bind without a dated Decision.
